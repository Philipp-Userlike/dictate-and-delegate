Privacy policy
Last updated: [DATE]

In short
Drift has no server and collects nothing. Your recordings and texts go from your phone straight to OpenRouter, with your own OpenRouter key, and from there to the model providers. Everything else stays on your phone.

What leaves your phone
When you stop recording, the audio goes over HTTPS to OpenRouter and on to the provider of the speech model you chose in Settings → Speech model, with your words as spelling hints. The transcript then goes the same way to a fast model that checks whether you asked Drift for something (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash). Only if you did, it goes on to the text model (Claude Sonnet 5.5), together with About you, your words, the template you asked for and what you attached (pasted text, images, PDFs). If you turned requests off in Settings → Requests, only the transcription happens.

Who receives it
OpenRouter, Inc. and the provider that serves each call (for example Microsoft Azure, OpenAI, AssemblyAI, DeepSeek or Anthropic). They process the data under their own terms and privacy policies, which may include processing in the United States, and they bill your OpenRouter account. Your OpenRouter settings decide, for example, whether providers that keep or train on data are allowed.

What stays on your phone
Your notes, both versions of each note, your words, your templates, About you, your OpenRouter key and your settings are stored only on this phone, encrypted where they contain your text or your key. Audio is kept for up to 7 days (at most 100 MB) in the app's private storage so you can redo a note with another model; you can turn this off or delete it in Settings. Attachments stay with their note for up to 7 days.

Permissions
Microphone: only while you record. Display over other apps (optional): the floating stop button, which also says when your text is ready to paste. Drift does not use notifications, Accessibility, screen recording, contacts or location, and it cannot read your screen.

Support emails
When you tap "Send feedback", Drift opens your email app with a prepared message containing the app version and your Android version. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.

Deleting your data
Settings → Delete all my data deletes everything Drift stored on this phone, including your key. Data at OpenRouter and the model providers is covered by their policies; your OpenRouter account shows and manages it.

Changes
We will update this page when Drift changes how it handles data.
