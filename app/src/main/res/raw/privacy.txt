Privacy
Talk Router is open source and has no server of its own. It has no account, no analytics and no crash reporting, and its developer receives none of your data. What you dictate goes from your phone straight to the services you set up, with your own keys.

What leaves your phone
• Your recording goes over HTTPS to OpenRouter and on to the provider of your speech model, with your words as spelling hints. The transcript goes the same way to Jev, a small decision model, with your template names and, if you said it, your chief's name.
• When a note is refined, the transcript, About you, your words, the template you named and your attachments go to the text model (Claude Sonnet 5.5): through OpenRouter, or straight to Anthropic if you saved an Anthropic key (through OpenRouter if Anthropic fails).
• If you connect bb, what you hand to your chief of staff, with text you pasted and images or PDFs you attached, goes to your own bb, via bb Connect (getbb.app) or the address you entered. Your chief sends notifications and text back through ntfy (ntfy.sh unless you entered your own server).

OpenRouter, Anthropic, the model providers and ntfy handle this data under their own terms and privacy policies, possibly in the United States. Your accounts there decide, for example, whether providers may keep data or train on it.

What stays on your phone
Notes, audio (up to 7 days, for redos), attachments, templates, words, About you, your keys, your bb sign-in and your settings. Texts and keys are encrypted and left out of backups. Settings → Delete local data removes all of it and signs out of bb.

Permissions
Microphone: only while you record. Display over other apps (optional): the floating panel. Accessibility (optional): only to put your text at the cursor when you tap Insert, and to notice when you hold the volume key you chose. Talk Router reads nothing on your screen and stores or sends nothing it sees there.
