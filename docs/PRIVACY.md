# Privacy and storage

The user-facing policy is `app/src/main/res/raw/privacy.txt` (German: `raw-de`). This page describes the implementation.

Talk Router has no server, analytics, crash reporting, accounts or screen reading. The network peers are OpenRouter (`OpenRouter.java`, base URL `BuildConfig.OPENROUTER_URL`) and, only if you connect it, your own bb and ntfy server.

- **What is sent:** after you stop, the audio goes over HTTPS to OpenRouter (`/audio/transcriptions`) in chunks of about two minutes, with your words as provider-specific spelling hints, and on to the host of the chosen speech model. The transcript then goes to Jev (`~typesafe/jev-latest`, `/api/alpha/decisions`) with the names of your templates and, only when your chief's name appears in it, that name. Only when you tap ✨ Refine, or Jev starts it, the transcript, About you, your words, the template asked for and the attachments go to the text model (Claude Sonnet 5.5, `/chat/completions`). Redos are sent the same way. No language setting is sent; the speech model detects it. Redirects are not followed. Requests carry `X-Title: Talk Router` for OpenRouter's app attribution.
- **Who sees it:** OpenRouter and the provider serving each call, under their policies and your OpenRouter account's settings (data retention, training, provider choice). Talk Router adds nothing in between.
- **bb (optional):** when you tap 🧑‍💼 Chief, or say by name that a dictation is for your chief, the transcript as said and pasted text from 📎 go to your bb through its MCP server (`BbBridge.java`): via bb Connect (`https://<handle>.getbb.app/mcp`, relayed by getbb.app to your computer) or a custom address you entered. Images and PDFs from 📎 are uploaded to your bb's attachments for Personal (`POST /api/v1/projects/proj_personal/attachments` on your bb, as bb's own app does) and go with the message; if bb refuses that upload, they go as base64 text through MCP. Talk Router installs the chief's two skills in your bb (`~/.bb/skills`, through a short bb thread): they hold your name if you entered one, your ntfy server and topic, and your bb link base. What the chief and its agents then do runs on your computer with the providers you chose in bb.
- **bb sign-in:** OAuth with PKCE against getbb.app (`BbOAuth.java`); the client ID is the public metadata document `oauth/client.json`. Access and refresh tokens, a custom bearer token and the ntfy topic are stored with AES-GCM in `Vault`. Sign out and Delete all my data revoke the tokens at getbb.app (best effort) and delete them.
- **ntfy:** the chief, on your computer, posts notifications and text to paste to `<server>/<topic>` (default ntfy.sh), and anyone who knows the topic can read them, so the topic is 24 random characters. The phone talks to ntfy when you tap **Send test**, while the panel waits for the chief's text (it reads the topic's JSON stream, from the moment the request was sent, until the text arrives or the panel closes), and when you tap **Copy** on the chief's notification (it fetches that request's text from the topic). The Copy broadcast carries only the request ID, never text, so another app can't put text on your clipboard this way. Talk Router itself posts no notifications and shows none.
- **Accessibility (optional):** `TalkAccessibility` requests no accessibility events (`res/xml/accessibility.xml` has no event types) and never reads the screen. It filters key events only to notice a hold of the volume key you chose (other keys pass straight through; a short press is re-sent as a volume change), and only when you tap Insert it looks up the focused text field (`findFocus(FOCUS_INPUT)`) to insert or, for Insert refined, to replace the transcript it inserted if that text is still there unchanged. Nothing it sees is stored or sent. Without the permission, Insert copies.
- **Anthropic (optional):** with your own Anthropic key (Settings → Refine → Anthropic key, checked with `GET /v1/models`, stored like the OpenRouter key), Refine's request (the transcript, About you, your words, the template and attachments) goes to `api.anthropic.com/v1/messages` (`Anthropic.java`, base URL `BuildConfig.ANTHROPIC_URL`) instead of OpenRouter, under Anthropic's policies. If that fails, the same request goes through OpenRouter.
- **The key:** your OpenRouter key is checked with `GET /key` and stored with AES-GCM under a non-exportable Android Keystore key (`Vault`), like templates and notes. It is sent only to OpenRouter, as a bearer token.
- **Notes:** encrypted in local SQLite; IDs and dates are plaintext. Backups and device transfer are excluded (`allowBackup="false"`, data extraction rules).
- **Audio:** kept by default for 7 days, at most 100 MB, oldest first, in the app's private no-backup directory, so a note can be redone with another speech model. Turning it off deletes saved audio. Audio of failed recordings is kept until retry or discard. Talk Router does not encrypt audio separately; device encryption applies.
- **Clipboard:** Talk Router writes it only when you ask: **Copy** on a note, **Copy** on the chief's notification, or when Insert can't reach a field (the panel says "Copied instead"). Insert sets the field's text through Accessibility; only for an app that accepts nothing but a paste does it go through the clipboard. Copied text is marked sensitive so Android hides previews. Clipboard managers and the app you paste into may keep it.
- **Screens:** the note list, notes and Activity are hidden from screenshots and Recents (`FLAG_SECURE`).
- **Delete all my data** clears notes, audio, attachments, templates, words, About you, the key, the bb sign-in, the ntfy topic and settings.

The repository holds no real keys, signing material or recordings. Screenshots use sample dictations.
