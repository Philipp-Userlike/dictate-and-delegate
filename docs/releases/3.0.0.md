# Talk Router 3.0.0

Talk over any app, put your words where you're typing, and hand real tasks to an agent on your own computer. In 2.1, Talk Router guessed what you wanted and put the result on your clipboard. In 3.0 your words come first and you decide, right where you are.

## New

- **Floating panel over any app.** After you talk, a small panel offers 📥 Insert, ✨ Refine and 🧑‍💼 Chief next to what you were doing. It never takes focus and stays where you drag it. Tap ✨ or 🧑‍💼 while still talking and it runs once the transcript is ready; ✕ throws a recording away at no cost.
- **Insert at the cursor.** Your words go straight into the text field you're in, through Android's Accessibility service.
- **Start with a volume key.** Hold volume down or up over any app to start and stop. A short press still changes the volume.
- **✨ Refine** replaces the text route: Claude Sonnet 5.5 turns what you said into the text you meant (message, email, reply, a template you named), and **Insert refined** swaps it in for the raw transcript. Ask for a text or name a template, and Refine starts by itself.
- **Refine with your own Anthropic key**, on your Anthropic credits, with OpenRouter as the fallback.
- **Call your chief by name.** Give your chief of staff a name and say "Ada, …": the dictation goes to your bb thread by itself, as you said it. Jev, a fast decision model, tells "Ada, book a table" apart from "say hi to Ada". Or just tap 🧑‍💼.
- **Text back from the chief.** When the chief writes something for you to paste, **Insert from chief** appears on the panel, or **Copy** on its notification.
- **Images and PDFs reach the chief.** What you attach with 📎 now goes along as attachments the chief can open, not only pasted text.
- **Skills installed for you.** The chief's instructions are now two bb skills, chief-of-staff and talk-router, that Talk Router installs in bb and keeps up to date by itself. The chief thread only needs one line.

## Changed

- **Your clipboard stays yours.** Talk Router no longer copies every dictation. It writes the clipboard only when you tap Copy, or when Insert can't reach a field (and says so).
- The floating button became the panel; the request check became Jev, which only starts things you'd clearly want.
- Activity shows each step of a note (speech, Jev, Refine, the hand-off to bb) with time and cost.

Still no Talk Router server or account: the phone talks to OpenRouter, Anthropic (optional), your bb and ntfy, with your own keys. Requires Android 14 or newer and an OpenRouter key. Updates install over 2.x.
