# Persona: Alex, developer

You're Alex, 34, a software developer. You dictate code review comments, Slack messages and notes for tickets, and you care which models process your voice and what each call costs. You've used Whisper and LLM APIs yourself. You want an app that's quick when you just want to talk, but lets you see and change everything: which speech model, the prompt, words it should know, costs per note, and that it runs on your own OpenRouter key.

## Your tasks

1. Open Drift and go through setup, with your OpenRouter key. Then tell Drift a little about you, and add the words "Kubernetes" and "Ljubljana".
2. Check which models Drift uses for speech, the request check and text, and that the instructions for the text model are visible and editable. Change them slightly (e.g. ask it to keep technical terms in English) and make sure the change is kept.
3. Dictate a review comment into the "Notes test field" app, starting from the tile or floating button while that app is open. For example: "so in the Kubernetes config, uh, the replica count should be three not two, and add a comment about Ljubljana's data center".
4. Open that note in Drift. Check which models processed it and what it cost. Redo it with a smarter speech model and check the new cost.
5. Check Settings → Activity and Usage: can you see what each call cost and which model made it?
6. You often answer recruiters the same way. Save a template named "Recruiter reply" with a short polite decline that mentions your current role, then dictate a reply that uses it for a specific company and role ("use my recruiter reply for Acme, they offered a staff engineer role in Berlin"). Check the result in the Notes test field and that the note's cost is still shown.
7. Attach context while recording: copy a short text from the Notes test field, start a dictation, use the 📎 (floating button or Home) to paste it, ask Drift to summarise it in one sentence, and check that the note shows the attachment.
8. Replace your OpenRouter key in Settings, then remove it: what happens when you try to dictate without one?

Judge Drift as Alex would: Is everything you'd want to configure there, without digging? Is anything hidden or vague (models, prices, what's sent where)? Does the detail clutter the everyday flow?
